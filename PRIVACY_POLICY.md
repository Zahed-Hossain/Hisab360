## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy outlines how **গাণিতিক ক্যালকুলেটর (Bangla Math Calculator)** handles your data and information when you use our Android application. We are committed to safeguarding user privacy and maintaining complete transparency regarding data processing practices.

---

## 2. Zero Personal Data Collection
Our application does not require any account creation, registration, login credentials, or personal identification. We **do not collect, process, or store** any of the following:
- Name, username, or alias
- Email address
- Phone number
- Physical address or precise geolocation (GPS)
- Contacts, SMS, call logs, or calendar events
- Photos, media files, or device documents
- Financial account details or payment methods

---

## 3. Calculator & Functional Data Processing
All calculations performed within the application (e.g., standard math, scientific functions, financial estimations, health indices, geometric conversions, age calculations) are computed entirely on your device using client-side logic.
- **Calculation History:** Calculation logs are stored exclusively in your device's private local storage.
- **Custom Formulas & Bookmarks:** User-created formulas and favorite tools remain entirely local.
- **Transmission:** No calculation inputs, variables, or outcomes are ever transmitted to our servers or any third-party backend.

---

## 4. Local Data Storage & User Control
The app utilizes your device's web storage (`localStorage`) solely to preserve:
- App visual preferences (Dark mode / Light mode, font size)
- Local calculation history
- User-defined math formulas
- User bookmarks

**Data Erasure & Reset:**  
You maintain complete ownership of this local data. You can delete individual calculation history items or perform a one-tap complete wipe via **Settings > ডাটা মুছে ফেলার অপশন > সব ডাটা ফ্যাক্টরি রিসেট করুন**. Uninstalling the app also permanently erases all local data.

---

## 5. Camera & QR Code Scanner
The application includes a QR Code Scanner and Maker.
- **Permission Use:** Camera access (`android.permission.CAMERA`) is requested **only** when you navigate to the QR Scanner tab or tap the camera activation button.
- **Ephemeral Processing:** Video frames are analyzed locally in memory in real time to decode QR codes and are immediately discarded.
- **No Capture or Upload:** The app does not record, take snapshots, store, or upload camera feeds or photos.
- **Zero-Camera Alternative:** Users can optionally scan QR codes by picking an image file from device storage, without granting camera permissions.

---

## 6. Currency Exchange Rate API
To provide real-time foreign currency conversions against the Bangladeshi Taka (BDT), the app queries the public Open Exchange Rates endpoint (`https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest/USD`).
- **Data Sent:** A standard HTTPS GET request fetching general exchange rate pairs. **No user identifiers, IP-linked accounts, or calculation values are submitted.**
- **Offline Fallback:** If internet connectivity is unavailable, the app relies on bundled offline exchange rates without interrupting functionality.

---

## 7. Google AdMob Advertising
To support continued development and maintenance, the application integrates the official **Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob)**.
- **Data Processed by Google AdMob:** In accordance with Google's policies and SDK operations, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:
  - Device and advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
  - IP address and general coarse location (country/city level)
  - Diagnostic, crash reporting, and ad performance logs
  - Fraud prevention and bot detection signals
- **Data Sharing:** AdMob data is processed directly by Google LLC under the [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Ad Unit Isolation:** Advertisements are rendered in separate native containers (`AdView`) and have zero access to your calculator's local storage or DOM.

## 8. Children's Privacy
Our application is a general-purpose mathematical and educational utility. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13.

## 9. Data Security
Because all calculations, notes, and history remain strictly on your local device, your data is protected by Android's application sandbox security model. All outbound network requests (for exchange rates) are strictly encrypted via TLS/HTTPS (`cleartextTrafficPermitted="false"`).

- **Personalized Ads:** You can manage or reset your Android Advertising ID at any time via **Android Settings > Google > Ads**.
- **Offline Use:** The entire application (all 17 calculator and utility modules) is 100% functional in airplane or offline mode.
